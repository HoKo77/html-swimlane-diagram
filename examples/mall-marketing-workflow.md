# Mall Admin Marketing Campaign Workflow

Generate a role-based swimlane diagram for a mall admin marketing campaign.

Suggested lanes:

- Marketing Operations
- Product / Inventory
- Design / Content
- Operations Manager
- Customer Service / Data

Suggested phases:

- Planning
- Configuration
- Review
- Launch
- Retrospective

Key flow:

1. Marketing operations initiates the campaign request.
2. Marketing operations defines campaign goals, budget, and promotion mechanics.
3. Marketing operations drafts the campaign plan with schedule, channels, and rules.
4. Product / inventory checks whether selected SKUs and stock are sufficient.
5. If stock is insufficient, marketing operations adjusts product or inventory selection and resubmits.
6. Marketing operations configures campaign rules in the mall admin system.
7. Design / content prepares campaign materials.
8. Operations manager reviews the campaign package.
9. If rejected, design / content revises according to feedback and resubmits.
10. Marketing operations launches or schedules the campaign.
11. Customer service prepares FAQ and after-sales handling rules.
12. Customer service / data monitors traffic, conversion, and support feedback.
13. Operations manager decides whether there is an anomaly.
14. If there is an anomaly, design / content or operations adjusts or pauses the campaign, then continues monitoring.
15. Marketing operations takes the campaign offline.
16. Customer service / data pulls retrospective metrics.
17. Operations manager records the retrospective and next strategy.
